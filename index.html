Section 03 — Scope

What $VAULT is not

This is the most important section on the page, so it is not written in small type at the bottom.

Not backingThe cards do not back the token. $VAULT is not a claim on the Vault or on anything in it.
Not redeemableThere is no mechanism, now or planned, to exchange tokens for a card or for its value.
Not a shareHolding $VAULT gives you no ownership of any card, no share of revenue, and no entitlement to creator fees.
Not a giveawayNo giveaway is running. Holding the token does not enter you into anything.
Not an investmentNo return is promised or implied. The token can go to zero, and most tokens like it do.
Not affiliatedNo connection to One Piece, Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Bandai, Toei, Shueisha, or any rights holder.

The Vault is a collection bought with project revenue and documented in public. That is the whole of it.

Section 04 — Mechanism

How it works

Four steps, then it repeats. The card is the content, and the content is the reason anyone looks.

STEP 01

Trade

Trading $VAULT generates creator fees on Pump.fun.

STEP 02

Split

Fees are split at platform level between the Vault wallet and operations. No manual transfer sits in between.

STEP 03

Acquire

The Vault buys a graded card. Transaction hash, price and certificate number are published here.

STEP 04

Show

The card becomes content. Content brings attention. Attention brings trading.

Card Vault is an experiment, not a financial product. Nothing here is investment advice. Cards held by the Vault are never sold except to acquire further cards for the Vault.
